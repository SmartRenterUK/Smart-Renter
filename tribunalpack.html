about your section 13 notice

The notice your landlord sent you is the starting point for any challenge. Let's check it's valid.

What is a Section 13 notice? It's the official form your landlord must use to propose a rent increase. It should be headed "Section 13 Notice" or reference "Form 4A." If your landlord just sent a letter or email saying the rent is going up, that may not be a valid notice.
⚠️ Your deadline is approaching. You need to submit your tribunal application before the effective date above. Don't wait — submit first and gather evidence afterwards.

step 4

your comparable properties

This is the most important evidence you can provide. Find similar properties in your area on Rightmove or Zoopla and add them below.

What to look for: Properties with the same number of bedrooms, in the same street or immediate area, listed or let within the last 6 months. Aim for at least 3. Rightmove and Zoopla screenshots are accepted by the tribunal.
