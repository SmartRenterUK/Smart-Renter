privacy policy

Last updated: April 2026

The short version: We only collect your email address when you sign up for early access. We don't sell it, share it with third parties, or use it for anything other than keeping you updated about Smart Renter. You can ask us to delete it any time.

who we are

Smart Renter is a UK-based platform that helps tenants understand their rights and navigate the rental process. Our website is smartrenter.co.uk.

For the purposes of UK data protection law, Smart Renter is the data controller responsible for your personal data.

what data we collect

At this stage, the only personal data we collect is your email address, which you provide voluntarily when signing up for early access to Smart Renter.

We do not collect:

  • Your name
  • Your address or postcode
  • Payment information
  • Any sensitive personal data

We may also collect standard website analytics data such as pages visited and time spent on site. This data is anonymised and cannot be used to identify you personally.

why we collect it

We collect your email address for one reason only — to let you know when Smart Renter launches and to share updates about the product. The legal basis for this processing is your consent, which you give when you submit the sign-up form.

how we store your data

Your email address is stored securely. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

We do not transfer your data outside the UK or EEA without appropriate safeguards in place.

who we share it with

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data to any third parties.

We may use a trusted third-party email service provider to send you updates. Any such provider is bound by strict data processing agreements and may only use your data to deliver emails on our behalf.

how long we keep it

We will keep your email address for as long as you remain signed up to receive updates from us. If you ask us to delete your data, we will do so promptly and in any case within 30 days.

your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the following rights in relation to your personal data:

  • Right of access — you can ask us what data we hold about you
  • Right to erasure — you can ask us to delete your data at any time
  • Right to rectification — you can ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Right to withdraw consent — you can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email we send you
  • Right to complain — you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk

cookies

Our website uses cookies in two ways. First, essential cookies that are required for the site to function. Second, analytics cookies from Google Analytics, which help us understand how visitors use the site — for example, which pages are most visited and how long people spend on them. This data is used only to improve Smart Renter and is not used to identify you personally.

We do not use advertising or targeting cookies. By continuing to use our site, you consent to our use of analytics cookies. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking at any time by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

changes to this policy

As Smart Renter grows and we add new features, we may update this privacy policy. We will always display the date it was last updated at the top of this page. If we make significant changes we will notify you by email.

contact us
